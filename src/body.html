VISION + AUDIO FUSIONCalibrate your neck, track the fretting hand (optional MediaPipe), and cross-check finger positions against what you actually played.

Tip: in CodePen open Change View → Full Page / Debug for best audio performance, allow microphone & camera access, and use headphones. With a Focusrite, set its buffer size in Focusrite Control (or your OS) as low as is stable, and prefer the interface's hardware Direct Monitor for zero-latency monitoring (mute the in-app monitor then).

On a phone or tablet: use headphones. In-app monitoring starts off to avoid speaker feedback (switch it on in ⋯ More). Landscape suits the Stage and Neural views, and ⋯ More ▸ Install app puts the-daily.studio on your home screen.
