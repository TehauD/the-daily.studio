# Licensing and provenance register

Audited for **v2.16.0** (31 October 2026). Every file the site serves, everything it can fetch, every dependency and every file a person can download is listed here with where it came from and what licence covers it. `npm run audit` checks this page against the real build and fails if a shipped file, a host or a dependency is not accounted for, so it stays true.

This is an engineering audit of the project, written by its maintainers. It is not legal advice. The items only a person can settle (authorship of the demo words, terms for a commercial launch, trademark clearance) are collected in [Open items](#open-items-for-the-maintainer).

The three questions, answered up front:

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is any bundled demo, sample, preset asset or downloadable output of unknown origin? | No. There is no bundled recording, no one else's lyrics and no preset sample or impulse response: tones, effects, the band and impulse responses are generated by code. The demo song is a short original. Downloads are built from what the person made or brought in. |
| Does anything in the dependency tree, the models, the fonts or the icons carry a restrictive licence? | No runtime dependencies at all. Two OFL fonts are embedded. Two optional MediaPipe models are fetched from Google after consent; their licence status is the one thing here that is *not* explicit (see [MediaPipe](#fetched-only-after-consent)). Development tools are permissive only. |
| Is "nothing leaves your device" accurate? | It is accurate for everything a person makes. The page can make network requests to exactly two other hosts, only after a yes. [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md) says so in full and `test/e2e-privacy.cjs` proves it. |

### Audio and presets

There are no audio files in the repository. Tones, amp and pedal models, the drum kit, bass, band patterns and room impulse responses are computed by code in `src/parts/` (a search for embedded binary data, base64 audio and numeric tables found none; `index.html` contains only the two fonts and one SVG as data). The "Neural Map" starts empty and learns during the session; it is not a trained model.

### The demo song

"Salt Wind (demo)" (`demoSong()` in `src/parts/01-core.js`) is two ordinary four-chord progressions (Am F C G and F C G Am) with four short lines of words. Chord progressions are common property. The words were written for this project. On 26 October 2026 the three most distinctive lines were searched on the web in quotation marks and nothing matched; that is a sanity check, not a clearance. See [Open items](#open-items-for-the-maintainer).

### No example songs; your pictures (2.6)

Version 2.6 ships no example songs (the six that 2.5 shipped, `src/parts/60-starters.js`, were removed). The only song words in the app are the four lines of the first-page demo song, above. The `samples/` shelf is empty and no audio is shipped.

The Show setup console lets a person add their own pictures. The app ships none. They are read in the page, reduced, kept in the browser's own database and never uploaded; SVG is refused. The Pictures tab says "Only use pictures you have the right to show", and what a person projects with them is their responsibility, as with their own recordings.

## Methods

YIN pitch estimation, harmonic/percussive median filtering and Karplus–Strong synthesis are re-implemented from their published papers (citations in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)); no code was copied. No patent search was done.

## What you bring in, and what comes out

The app ships no music and has no catalogue, no web import, no fingerprinting and no download of songs. Whatever recordings, lyrics, MIDI and tab are in the app were put there by the person using it, and stay on their device ([PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md)). Because the app can split, transcribe and share material, the rights depend on the source, the processing and where the result goes:

| Output | Made from | What it contains | Rights note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ultimate Guitar text, ChordPro, lyrics only, tab only | The song on the page: the person's chords, lyrics and tab, or ones extracted from a file they loaded | Chords, words, tab | Your own song: yours. A chart, words or tab of someone else's song is based on their work: check the rights holder and the site you post to |
| MIDI, Project JSON | The song on the page | Notes and settings | As above |
| Bounce WAV, each band part, all takes, compressed audio (M4A / Opus) | The song, the person's recorded takes, and any backing track or stems they loaded | Sound | Contains the loaded track if there is one. Share only what you have the right to share |
| Share page (one HTML file) | The song as recorded from the master output, with chords and lyrics | Sound, words, chords in one file | Same. The footer reads "The words and music belong to their authors" |
| Print / PDF sheet | The song's chords and lyrics | Words and chords | Same as the text exports |
| Sky poster (PNG) | The session's chord constellation | Title, key and chord names | The person's own session |
| Stage video (.webm / .mp4) | The visuals and the song's sound | Picture and sound | Contains the loaded track if there is one |
| Backups and song files (zip) | Everything in the library | Songs, takes, loaded tracks, stems | A private copy for the person; sharing it shares what is inside |

How the app helps people stay on the right side of this, without getting in the way:

- Short notices where material comes in or goes out: the Stems panel ("Use music you made or have the right to use…"), the lyric importer, the Export panel (both the chart and the file buttons) and the share-page footer. The About panel's "Your music" line says the same.
- Stems, chords and tab are described honestly as derived from the loaded file. **Lyrics are not extracted from audio.** The app only uses lyrics a person types, pastes or opens as a text file.
- Nothing is sent to anyone for the app to work, so the app itself never distributes the material; the person does, when they choose to share a file.

## Open items for the maintainer

These are decisions or facts only the project's owner can confirm. None blocks the build.

1. **Demo words.** Confirm you wrote (or commissioned) the four lyric lines in `demoSong()`. They are described as original throughout; the web check found no match but is not a clearance.
2. **Model terms.** Before any commercial or self-hosted use of `selfie_segmenter.tflite` (and for the hand model, to be certain), read the terms on the model cards linked from Google's MediaPipe pages, or ask in the MediaPipe repository. Today the project does not host either file.
3. **A terms-of-use page.** The MIT licence covers the code and its warranty disclaimer; it says nothing about a hosted service. If the site becomes a public product, consider a short terms page (use at your own risk; you are responsible for the material you load) and a contact for takedown requests.
4. **Authorship note.** The code was developed with AI assistance under your direction (stated in About). Copyright in AI-assisted work depends on the human contribution and differs by country; the MIT notice grants whatever rights exist. A lawyer can say how that affects a commercial launch.
5. **Names.** "Ultimate Guitar", "Focusrite" and "Scarlett" appear only to describe compatibility and carry a not-affiliated notice. No trademark search was done for `the-daily.studio` or "The Daily".
6. **Cloudflare Web Analytics.** Leave its automatic script injection off. If it is ever turned on, the security policy will block the script and `test/e2e-privacy.cjs` will fail against the live site, and the privacy statement must change first.
7. **The Daily's policy.** Its page allows connections to GitHub, Azure DevOps, OpenAI and Azure OpenAI only (plus localhost), while its code also names other AI providers; those calls are blocked on this site. Its policy also allows images from any `https` address. Both are noted in [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md).

## Keeping this true

- Adding a file to the site, a dependency, a font, a sample or a new host means adding a row here, in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and (for a host) in `PRIVACY.md` and the security policy in `scripts/build.mjs`, in the same change. `npm run audit` and `npm run verify:site` fail until they agree.
- Sample tracks go in `samples/` only with their licence written in `samples/LICENSE.md`, one line per file.
- Run `test/e2e-privacy.cjs` before a release and against the live site after a deploy: `BASE_URL=https://the-daily.studio/ node test/e2e-privacy.cjs`.
